  • Możesz usunąć swój wpis w każdej chwili przyciskiem „Opuść lot i usuń moje dane". Wiadomości i prośby z już zakończonych zamian wygasają wtedy automatycznie w terminie wskazanym wyżej.
  • Anonimowy identyfikator pozostaje na Twoim urządzeniu do odinstalowania aplikacji lub wyczyszczenia jej danych.

SeatSwap – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

SeatSwap (the "app") helps passengers on the same flight agree on swapping seats. This page explains what data we process, why, and for how long.

1. Data controller

Radosław Kij, Humniska 652a, 36-206 Humniska , e-mail: crptkj@gmail.com

2. What data we process

DataPurpose
An anonymous user ID (Firebase Authentication). It is not linked to your name, email address or phone number.To tell which entry, request and message belongs to which user.
First name (or nickname), seat number, optional note and group code, a flag "seat confirmed with boarding pass", flight number and flight dateTo show your seat to other passengers on the same flight and make swaps possible.
Swap requests (names, seats, optional comment, status) and chat messages (text, name, time)To arrange a swap between two people. Only the participants of that swap can see them.
User reports (reporter and reported user IDs, name, optional reason, time)To respond to abuse and false information.
Technical data processed by Google (e.g. IP address, Firebase installation ID)To operate Firebase services (anonymous sign-in, database).

Boarding pass. If you choose a boarding pass screenshot, the app reads the barcode or QR code only on your phone. The image is never uploaded or stored. From the code we use only the flight number, date, route and seat to check it matches the flight and to confirm your seat. The passenger name on the pass is not read or stored. To scan a flight QR code the app uses Google's scanner (Google Play services), which does not send the image to us.

On your device we store settings (theme, language), your last flight, your list of blocked users and read markers for chats. These never leave your phone.

The app has no ads, no analytics, and does not sell data. We do not collect location, contacts or photos.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app functions you ask for) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest: security and abuse prevention).

4. Recipients

Data is stored by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC as a processor (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Your name, seat and note are visible to other users on the same flight, and chat content to the other party of the swap. Data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area under the standard contractual clauses used by Google.

5. Retention

  • Passenger entries, the seat registry, requests, messages and flight data are deleted automatically about 3 days after the scheduled departure.
  • User reports are deleted after about 30 days.
  • You can delete your entry at any time with "Leave flight and delete my data". Messages and requests from finished swaps then expire automatically within the period above.
  • The anonymous ID stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because the data is pseudonymous, please include the flight number, date and your nickname so we can find it. Contact: [YOUR_EMAIL].

7. Children

The app is not intended for people under 16.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and database access is restricted by security rules. Confirming a seat with a boarding pass reduces mistakes and abuse but does not guarantee that other users' data is truthful.

9. Changes

We will announce changes by updating this page and the date above.
